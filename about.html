The Meaning of XELVARON ✨

Xelvaron is an acronym for Xelerate Valiant Resonate, proudly representing the spirit of class X.5. Guided by the symbol of a five-pointed star, we are a collective driven to move swiftly (Xelerate), stand brave against obstacles (Valiant), and radiate a positive echo that inspires society (Resonate).

"Like a star in the night sky, Xelvaron is bound to shine and lead the way."

Our Official Color

Brown teaches us to stay humble and strong, while silver inspires us to shine and reach our dreams. Brown represents loyalty, comfort, and stability — like a strong foundation that keeps us together. Silver represents creativity, success, and a future full of possibilities. As a class, we believe these two colors perfectly reflect our journey: growing together, supporting one another, and continuing to shine no matter where life takes us.

The Guide of Our Journey

Our beloved wali kelas, Pak Amir Hamzah, has taught us not only lessons from books, but also lessons about respect, discipline, and togetherness. He is the guiding star that keeps X.5 on the right track.
